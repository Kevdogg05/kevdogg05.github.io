About Us

Born on the Road

Road Queen Fitness started where every soca lover comes alive — on the road. Hours of chipping, wining and jumping behind the music truck take real stamina, real strength and real joy. We built a training experience that gives you all three.

Every class fuses authentic Soca and Caribbean rhythm with proven conditioning: HIIT intervals timed to the drop, lower-body sculpting that builds the hips and core you wine with, and choreography that turns sweat into celebration.

Training for your first mas band or your fifteenth Carnival Monday? You'll find a community of queens who hype each other up — on Zoom, on Instagram Live and on the road.

  • RhythmEvery rep on the beat — soca, dancehall and afrobeats.
  • ConditioningReal strength and cardio built for eight-hour road marches.
  • RoyaltyConfidence, community and zero judgment, every class.

Our Goals

What every queen trains for

  1. 01

    Road-ready stamina

    Build the endurance to chip, jump and wine from J'ouvert morning to the last lap.

  2. 02

    Strength that moves

    Sculpt glutes, legs and core with conditioning that serves the dance floor and the road.

  3. 03

    Master the moves

    Break choreography down step by step until every wine, roll and jump feels natural.

  4. 04

    A queendom, not a class

    Grow a worldwide community that trains together, travels together and celebrates each other.
