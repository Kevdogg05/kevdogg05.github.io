## Before you post

- **Add music in Instagram.** Pick a trending sound from the Reels audio library. Licensed in-app audio gets more reach than audio baked into the file.
- **Set the cover.** Tap "Edit cover", then "Add from camera roll", and choose `cover.jpg`.
- **Post before Halloween.** The coffin box is a seasonal hook, so it lands best this week.
