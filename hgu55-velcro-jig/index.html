HGU-55/P battery pack Velcro jig

Which rear jig to print

START HERE

Universal (flat, flexible)

It prints flat and bends onto the back of any of the three shells. Bending doesn't stretch it, so the 4 in window and the 1-1/2 in offset stay exact along the shell on every size.

Pre-curved M, L, XL

Curved to the estimated shell of each size, so they sit on the helmet without being held flat. No HGU-55/P shell measurements are published, so these curves are estimates. The jigs are thin enough to flex the last millimetre or two onto the real shell.

How it works

Using the rear jig

  1. Find the rear centreline. Measure along the edge roll between matching hardware on the left and right sides of the shell. Mark the halfway point with a grease pencil or masking tape.
  2. Seat the jig. Set its bottom edge down on top of the edge roll, with the V-notch and arrow on your mark. Hold it with a rubber band or strap through the end slots, or with painter's tape.
  3. Apply the pile. Cut it 2 × 4 in, peel the backing, and set its bottom edge on the ledge between the posts. Its top edge meets the ticks on the posts. Press it down.
  4. Remove the jig and press the patch down firmly.

Prepare the surface and handle the adhesive as the T.O. directs. Check your first install against the T.O. with a ruler before you use the jig for production.

Cord patches (1 × 1-1/2 in): hold the stencil where the T.O. puts each patch and press the tape through the window. Battery pack (3-3/4 × 2 in hook): lay the template on the back of the pack, even out the margins around the frame, and press the strip in through the window.

Print settings

  • PETG is best because it flexes without cracking. PLA is fine for the pre-curved jigs and the templates.
  • Use 0.2 mm layers and 3 walls, so every 2 mm section prints solid. No supports for any part.
  • Print the universal jig and the templates flat, as exported. The smooth side on the bed touches the helmet.
  • Print M, L and XL standing on the bottom edge, as exported, with a 5–8 mm brim.
  • Check the first print: 103.1 mm between the posts, and 38.1 mm from the bottom edge to the ledge.

Helmet sizes and curvature

Gentex makes the HGU-55/P shell in Medium, Large and X-Large. Its manual (TP0351, Table 2-1) sizes helmets by the wearer's head. Gentex doesn't publish the shell's outside dimensions or curvature, so the shell shapes below are estimated from the head sizes plus about 25 mm of liner and shell.

Over the area the jig covers, one size to the next changes the shape by about 1 mm (Medium to X-Large: 2 mm at the band, 4 mm at the post tips). The patch size and offset are the same for every size, so one thin jig that bends covers all three. The uncertainty in the estimate is bigger than the difference between sizes.

To make a pre-curved jig from measurements of your own helmets, see the README. It takes a steel rule and two gap measurements, then one command:
